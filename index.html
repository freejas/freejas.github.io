About

I am a behavioral scientist working at the intersection of social-ecological, cross-cultural, and evolutionary psychology. My research asks how our social environment shapes — and is in turn shaped by — our individual psychology, with a focus on intergroup processes and relationship formation.

I am currently a Post-Doctoral Assistant Professor at the Polish Academy of Sciences in Warsaw (April 2026 – June 2027), where I work in the Macropsychology Lab with Kuba Krys. I completed my PhD in Behavioral Science at Hokkaido University in Sapporo, Japan, in March 2025; my dissertation examined relational mobility as a socio-ecological foundation of prosocial minds and behaviors. Before coming to Warsaw, I was a Post-Doctoral Research Fellow at the University of Sussex (April 2025 – February 2026) on the HONORLOGIC project with Ayse Uskul.
